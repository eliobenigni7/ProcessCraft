# Code of Conduct

ProcessCraft adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
channel where people take part in the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus criticism on the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and
personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, write to eliobenigni7@gmail.com with "ProcessCraft
conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer
decides on consequences following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private
warning to a ban from the project.
